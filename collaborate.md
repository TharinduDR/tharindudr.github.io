### Prospective PhD Students

I welcome enquiries from self-motivated prospective PhD students whose research interests align with mine. However, **I do not currently have funded PhD positions available**. Any funded PhD studentships will be advertised on this website and through my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/).

If you are **self-funded** or planning to apply for **external funding**, you are welcome to contact me to discuss potential supervision.

For international applicants considering funding schemes such as Commonwealth Scholarships or other government-sponsored scholarships, please review the relevant eligibility criteria and application procedures before getting in touch. I am happy to discuss potential supervision where there is a strong research fit, but I cannot guarantee funding or institutional nomination.

**When contacting me, please:**

- Briefly introduce yourself and your research interests.
- Identify **three of my publications** that motivated you to get in touch.
- Explain briefly how these publications relate to the research you would like to pursue.
- Indicate whether you are self-funded or intend to apply for a specific funding scheme.

Please note that generic enquiries that do not demonstrate a clear connection to my research may not receive a response.

### Prospective Visiting Researchers

I welcome enquiries from researchers interested in short-term research visits to Lancaster University, particularly where there are opportunities for collaboration on shared research interests.

Please note that **I do not have dedicated funding available to support visiting researchers**. Visitors are generally expected to secure their own funding or support through their home institutions.

For eligible researchers affiliated with relevant COST Actions, we may be able to explore **Short-Term Scientific Mission (STSM) grants** to support a research visit, subject to the funding rules, availability, and approval of the relevant COST Action.

If you are interested in visiting, please briefly introduce your research background, proposed collaboration, intended duration of the visit, and any funding arrangements you have in place.
