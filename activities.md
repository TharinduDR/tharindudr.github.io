My professional activities include the organisation of conferences, workshops and shared tasks, editorial service, reviewing and area chairing, and invited academic talks and tutorials.

## Conference and Workshop Organisation

- **LoResLM Workshop Series ** — Founding co-organiser of the Workshop on Language Models for Low-Resource Languages.
- **EAMT 2023** — Research Track Chair, 24th Annual Conference of the European Association for Machine Translation.

## Shared Task Organisation

I have co-organised shared tasks covering offensive language identification and multilingual lexical simplification.

- **HASOC (2021–2023)** — Co-organiser of the shared task on Hate Speech and Offensive Content Identification in English and Indo-Aryan Languages.
- **Multilingual Lexical Simplification Pipeline (MLSP), BEA 2024** — Co-organiser of a multilingual shared task that brings multiple stages of the lexical simplification pipeline together within a single evaluation setting.

## Area Chairing and Reviewing

I regularly serve as an **Area Chair for major NLP conferences through ACL Rolling Review (ARR) cycles**.
